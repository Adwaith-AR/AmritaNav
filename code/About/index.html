ABOUT AMRITANAV

Your Campus. One Place.

AmritaNav is a student-focused campus platform designed to make navigating and experiencing campus easier.

01

Why AmritaNav?

A large campus can be confusing, especially when you're new. Finding a classroom, locating a department, discovering food options or finding important contacts shouldn't require searching through multiple places.

AmritaNav brings essential campus information together in one simple and accessible platform.

Make navigating and experiencing campus easier for every student.

Navigate Smarter

Find the places and information you need without wasting time searching.

02

What You Can Find

Campus Navigation

Find classrooms, departments and important locations around campus.

Food Courts

Explore food options, locations, menus and timings in one place.

Student Clubs

Discover student clubs and opportunities to get involved in campus life.

Announcements

Stay updated with important campus news, events and notices.

Emergency

Quickly access important emergency contacts and support services.

Student Resources

Keep useful academic and campus resources within easy reach.
